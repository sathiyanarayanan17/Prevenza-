What a result looks like

An invented message, checked by the same engine you use on the checker page.

SBI ALERT: Your KYC is pending. Your account will be blocked today. Share the OTP you received to update.

High risk Warning level 70 of 100. This is a warning level, not a probability.
  • Asks you to share an OTP, PIN or password
  • Says your KYC is pending or must be updated
  • Warns that your account, card or SIM will be blocked

Prevy says: Do not enter payment details, passwords or codes until you have verified this independently with the real organisation or someone you trust.

Know the tricks

Common scams in India

Scammers create panic so you act before you think. These are the ones Prevenza looks for.

  • Fake KYC update
  • "Digital arrest" calls
  • Parcel re-delivery fee
  • Lottery or prize win
  • Fake customer care numbers
  • "Pay first to receive money" UPI requests
  • Asking for your OTP or UPI PIN
  • Fake payment screenshots
  • Look-alike bank and shop websites
  • Asking you to move money to a "safe account"

Your data stays with you

Privacy

Everything you check is analysed inside your own browser or phone.

  • The page's security policy blocks all network connections (connect-src 'none'). Even a bug could not send what you checked anywhere.
  • Screenshots are read on your device by bundled offline text reading. The image and its text are not saved.
  • Only your settings (language, Elder Mode) and counts of checks by result level are kept, in this browser's local storage. You can reset the counts on the checker page.
  • Links, messages, UPI details, names, amounts and screenshot text are never stored.
  • No account, no cookies set by this site, no analytics, no adverts.

Please read

Honest limits

Prevenza is a helper, not a guarantee.

  • It follows fixed rules for common scam patterns. New wording, other languages and brand-new scams can be missed.
  • A low result means no strong warning signs were found. It never means something is safe or genuine.
  • The warning level is not a probability. Prevenza has not measured how accurate it is.
  • It cannot see your bank or UPI app, check who owns a UPI ID, or stop, cancel or recover a payment.
  • A payment screenshot is never proof of payment. Confirm it in your own bank or UPI app.
  • Hindi and Tamil text needs review by native speakers. Detailed reasons are in English.
