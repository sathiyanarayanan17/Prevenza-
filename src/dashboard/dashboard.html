      Type the details of a payment you are about to make, or were asked to make. They are checked on your device. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Prevenza cannot see your UPI app, and it cannot stop or cancel a payment.

      Check a screenshot

      Choose a screenshot you saved yourself, such as a chat or a payment receipt. Prevenza reads the text on your device. The image and the text are never saved or sent anywhere. Prevenza cannot see WhatsApp, your bank or your UPI app, and it never takes screenshots by itself.

      Do not trust a payment screenshot alone. Verify the payment in your own bank or UPI application.
