    Your data

    Prevenza keeps only your settings and two counters on this device. It never stores web addresses, page text, passwords or payment details.

    Privacy and permissions

    • The only browser permission is storage, to remember these settings.
    • On web pages Prevenza reads only the site name (for example, example.com). It never reads what you type.
    • No passwords, payment details, cookies or page text are collected.
    • Nothing is sent over the internet. The extension is blocked from making network requests.
